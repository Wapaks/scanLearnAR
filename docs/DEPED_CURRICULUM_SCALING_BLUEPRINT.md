# ScanLearn DepEd Curriculum Scaling Blueprint

## Purpose

This document defines the curriculum-first scaling direction for ScanLearn using the DepEd grade-level standards shared for Grades 1 to 6.

This blueprint should guide:

- curriculum planning
- quarter expansion
- lesson writing
- mission design
- assessment coverage
- scan-supported enrichment
- future teacher publishing workflows

This is the scaling layer that should sit above the technical LMS architecture already built in the app.

## Scope Rule

This blueprint covers:

- Grade 1
- Grade 2
- Grade 3
- Grade 4
- Grade 5
- Grade 6

Kindergarten is intentionally excluded for now.

## Product Rule

When ScanLearn scales, it should follow this sequence:

`DepEd Grade Standard -> Quarter Focus -> Units -> Lessons -> Activities -> Assessment -> Mastery -> Scan Enrichment`

Not:

`Interesting object -> scanner content -> random quiz`

## Scaling Principles

### 1. Curriculum First

Every quarter must be based on a DepEd-aligned academic focus, not only on what the scanner can detect.

### 2. Scanner As Enrichment

Scanning should support observation-heavy lessons, classification tasks, and concrete examples.

Scanning should not define the curriculum by itself.

### 3. One Grade At A Time

Do not build all grades in parallel.

Recommended rollout after Grade 3 Quarter 1 stabilization:

1. Finish Grade 3 Quarters 2 to 4
2. Build Grade 4
3. Then scale down to Grades 1 and 2, or up to Grades 5 and 6 depending on pilot needs

### 4. Four Quarter Structure

Every grade should be planned as:

- Quarter 1
- Quarter 2
- Quarter 3
- Quarter 4

Even if only one quarter is implemented at first.

## Grade 1

### Grade-Level Standard Summary

At the end of Grade 1, learners use their senses to observe their body, animals, plants, objects, weather, sounds, and movement. They begin describing similarities, differences, safety practices, and simple ways of interacting with the environment.

### Recommended Quarter Map

#### Quarter 1: My Body And My Senses

Focus areas:

- external body parts
- five senses
- simple body functions
- describing things using the senses

Target competency directions:

- identify major body parts
- name the senses and match them to what they do
- describe objects using color, size, texture, taste, and sound

Possible scan-supported lesson clusters:

- body-part matching cards
- classroom object observation
- texture and color object hunts

#### Quarter 2: Animals, Plants, And Everyday Objects

Focus areas:

- common animals
- common plants
- object shape and color
- similarities and differences

Target competency directions:

- compare two objects or living things
- describe simple features of animals and plants
- classify familiar items by visible traits

Possible scan-supported lesson clusters:

- leaves and flowers
- birds, pets, insects
- classroom material categories

#### Quarter 3: Movement, Sound, And Weather

Focus areas:

- how things move
- sounds in the environment
- daily weather conditions

Target competency directions:

- describe movement
- differentiate sound sources
- describe weather and related daily actions

Possible scan-supported lesson clusters:

- moving objects
- sound source prompts
- weather-linked outdoor observation cards

#### Quarter 4: Safety, Habits, And Simple Making

Focus areas:

- healthy habits
- personal safety
- asking questions and describing observations
- simple making and sorting tasks

Target competency directions:

- practice healthy and safe behavior
- sort and group simple materials
- describe what was observed or made

Possible scan-supported lesson clusters:

- hygiene objects
- safe versus unsafe household items
- school bag and home object sorting

## Scan-Supported Scaling Strategy

As the curriculum grows, scan-supported lessons should fit these categories best:

- plant observation
- animal classification
- classroom and household material study
- living versus non-living classification
- force and motion examples
- weather and preparedness objects
- body-health support materials

Scan support will be weaker for:

- abstract body systems
- reproductive processes
- moon patterns and astronomy concepts
- energy transformation theory
- ecosystem systems thinking

For those, the app should rely more on:

- lesson player content
- teacher-guided missions
- assessments
- diagrams and curated visuals

## Recommended Expansion Order

### Immediate

- stabilize Grade 3 Quarter 1
- finish Grade 3 QA and release validation

### Next

- build Grade 3 Quarter 2
- then Grade 3 Quarter 3
- then Grade 3 Quarter 4

### After Full Grade 3

Recommended next grade:

- Grade 4

Reason:

- it naturally continues the current Grade 3 developmental level
- it still works well with observation-heavy science
- it strengthens the curriculum-first progression before moving into the more abstract Grade 5 and Grade 6 topics

## Implementation Rule For Future Seeding

For every new grade and quarter, build in this order:

1. DepEd standard summary
2. quarter focus
3. units
4. lessons
5. competencies
6. activities
7. assessments
8. missions
9. scan-supported clusters
10. section release plan

## Final Guidance

This blueprint should be treated as the curriculum scaling source of truth for Grades 1 to 6.

The app is already architected to support:

- multiple grades
- four quarters per grade
- quarter-aware progress
- lesson-aware missions
- teacher-controlled publishing

So the right next step is not to build all grades at once.

The right next step is:

- use this blueprint to validate the Grade 3 path
- finish Grade 3 first
- then expand quarter by quarter using DepEd-aligned structure
